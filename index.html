Auto-Pause & Instant Resume.

Keep your hands on the keyboard. Toggle on Auto-Pause and the video automatically pauses and prepends a clickable timestamp the instant you start typing a new note line. Hit ⇧⌘Space (Ctrl+Shift+Space on Windows/Linux, or Ctrl+Space inside the editor) to immediately resume playback without losing cursor focus.

Markdown & Offline-First.

Write in the format you already know. NoteFlow parses full GitHub-flavored Markdown. Because your notes are stored in native browser storage, you can write and review them completely offline.
