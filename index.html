Markdown & Offline-First.

Write in the format you already know. NoteFlow parses full GitHub-flavored Markdown. Because your notes are stored in native browser storage, you can write and review them completely offline.

Bring Your Own Sync.

No proprietary cloud lock-in. Configure a custom server endpoint to sync your notes to your own database, Notion, or Obsidian vaults instantly via API. Your notes, your infrastructure.

Lightweight

Zero heavy frameworks injection into page content. Built on native DOM APIs and browser storage to ensure YouTube keeps playing flawlessly.

Secure by Design

Strict CSP compliance out of the box. Extension requests no invasive permissions beyond what is absolutely required to operate the side panel.

Instantly Exportable

Never trap your data. Download your notes as raw .md files or copy the entire document to your clipboard with a single click.
